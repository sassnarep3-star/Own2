How to use this ledger

  1. Pick a category — skill tournaments, trivia, fantasy & sports picks, creative contests, forecasting, market competitions, or tech & data contests.
  2. Check the free path — green means a completely free way to play exists; amber means only some events are free; red means cash play requires an entry fee.
  3. Verify on the official page — click the official source link in each row and read the current rules before entering. Prizes and eligibility change.
  4. Watch the flags — Caution, Ended, Mixed, and Unverified entries are listed on the Flagged for Review page.

Data reviewed 2026-08-19 from the Free Money & Contest Ledger (pass S). This is a research desk, not a sponsor. Official rules win every argument. US prizes of $600+ typically require a W-9; taxes are the winner’s responsibility. Nobody legitimate asks you to pay a fee to collect a prize.
