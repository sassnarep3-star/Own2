Executive decision

For an AI coding-agent workflow, the best current fits are Codex x TwitchCon (explicit Codex requirement, but deadline is 2026-11-02), Kiroween (Kiro required, public repo, Dec. 4), PayPal AI Hackathon (PayPal + AI, Nov. 12), UGREEN AI NAS (constrained Skill workflow, Nov. 15), and Codegeist (Forge/MCP/agent tracks, Dec. 22). Do not rank by headline prize alone. Confirm the live form, individual eligibility, required tools, IP/publicity terms, and whether the required demo can be completed before the deadline. No expected-earnings estimate is made because win probability and judging outcomes are not verified.
