Verification standard

Each populated factual field is tied to a retrieved organizer/administrator source. Editorial fit and recommendation are labeled separately. Link retrieval is not a continuous availability monitor.

  • Official source means the organizer, sponsor, administrator, or government challenge page—not the reference ledger or a review blog.
  • Every populated factual field in the table is backed by at least one evidence item in evidence.json.
  • Blank means not confirmed from the retrieved official source. It does not mean zero, none, or unavailable.
  • Cash totals are sums of official USD prize rows where stated; credits, subscriptions, hardware, swag, and travel are not counted as cash.
  • AI fit is an editorial assessment. Explicit tool requirements and permissions are quoted separately.
  • This pass does not exhaustively verify all 206 inherited ledger entries, all external links, Texas-specific legal eligibility, tax treatment, or future rule changes.
