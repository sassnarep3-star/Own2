## Decision brief

- **Build-first lead:** [PayPal AI Hackathon official rules](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules) say entry needs no payment, first place is **USD $12,000**, submission period ends **12 November 2026 at noon Pacific Time**, and the product must integrate PayPal's free sandbox and an AI tool. They do **not expressly say a coding agent may author the submission**. The entrant must comply with third-party licences and submit a functioning project/demo. Residency and local-law restrictions apply; Singapore-specific approval is not explicit. Do not equate the rules page's 12:00 Pacific time with a separately displayed 3:00 Eastern label without accounting for date and DST.
- **Bot-specific lead:** [FutureEval Fall 2026](https://www.metaculus.com/tournament/fall-futureeval-2026/) is explicitly **bot-only**, advertises a **$50,000 prize pool** and closes **6 January 2027**. [General Metaculus rules](https://www.metaculus.com/tournament-rules/) say no entry fee, bots normally cannot win **unless event rules specifically permit them**, and prizes may be paid in **cash or cash equivalents**. The [operator's bot resource](https://www.metaculus.com/notebooks/38928/ai-benchmark-resources/) confirms a $50k bot season. **Cash-payout cell is blank**: the $50k pool is not guaranteed withdrawable money. Computational costs and Singapore-specific payouts are unconfirmed.
- **Creative leads:** [Sony Single Image](https://www.worldphoto.org/sony-world-photography-awards/single-image) is free for up to three 2026 photographs, with **USD $5,000** for the overall winner by **5 January 2027 at 16:00 GMT**; [Sony Series](https://www.worldphoto.org/sony-world-photography-awards/series) is free for five to ten images, age >18, overall **USD $25,000**, due **12 January 2027 at 16:00 GMT**. Both require an original photograph, not computer-generated content. [Wergle Flomp](https://winningwriters.com/our-contests/wergle-flomp-humor-poetry-contest-free) has **no fee**, **$5,100 total prizes**, due **1 April 2027**; **AI-generated poem text is prohibited** and any AI use must be disclosed. These are human-creative competitions, not suitable for agent-generated final output.

## Risks and irregularities

No official page inspected grants personalized Singapore prize eligibility or payment approval; published geography rules are not legal advice. The PayPal event header presents a different time-zone label from its rules; **the rules' date/time is the recorded value**. Metaculus bot prize permission is event-specific, not a platform-wide authorization, and cash versus cash-equivalent payout remains unresolved. Sony manipulation and Wergle AI policies make automatic final-content creation hazardous. All five rows have a source and a dated review note in [Flagged for Review](flagged.html). No accounts, entries, payments, or win-probability estimates were made. Recheck the live operator terms immediately before deciding to participate.
