The executive summary

This session’s initial checkout contained only a README; other sessions independently published the root dashboard, a focused shortlist and an audit supplement during this work. All are retained. This is a complementary view, not a replacement. The supplied ledger contains 206 listings across nine categories, plus 45 historical exclusions. This dashboard retains all 206 listings and adds 10 event-level records from current official pages. It is a research inventory, not a list of 216 verified free-cash contests.

6 records have both free entry and a cash award supported by official evidence. Three additional Metaculus events have free entry and advertised monetary pools, but official rules permit cash-equivalent payments. They are excluded from the strict free + cash view. Payment method, eligibility and tool permissions remain separate checks.

Where to spend research time first

These are editorial priorities based on explicit AI/tool rules and concrete deliverables, not a prediction of winning or an estimate of hourly earnings. Fees, compute costs, chances of winning and time-to-complete are not invented.

1. PayPal AI Hackathon — a focused software build

Official rules require a working PayPal sandbox + AI application and say no purchase or payment is necessary. First place is $12,000 USD. Submission period ends 12 November 2026 at 12:00 Pacific Time. A demo and required developer accounts are needed. Official rules ↗

Recommended approach: choose one narrow user problem, validate a sandbox transaction flow, and build an end-to-end demo with a reproducible README. This recommendation is an editorial workflow, not an official scoring guarantee.

2. FutureEval — direct fit for a forecasting bot, with a payout caveat

The Fall 2026 event is bot-only, advertises a $50,000 pool and closes 6 January 2027. Official resources say to submit only one forecast per question in the bot-only series. General tournament rules permit cash-equivalent payouts, so withdrawable cash is not guaranteed. Event ↗ Bot resources ↗ Payment rules ↗

Recommended approach: first establish a logged baseline, measure calibration, and budget API use. Do not assume free entry means free compute or automated onboarding.

3. NFL Big Data Bowl 2027 — bounded analytics work

Rules state no purchase is necessary and list a $100,000 pool across tracks and a final presentation prize. External models and AutoML are conditionally permitted. The deadline is 6 January 2027 at 23:59 UTC; the writeup must link Combine sensor data to NFL performance. Rules ↗ Deliverables ↗

Recommended approach: investigate one position or drill, test a defensible linkage, and show uncertainty rather than claiming a broad scouting solution.

Important exclusions and restrictions

  • GamerSaloon’s advertised $10 is Bonus Cash and cannot be withdrawn. Official promotion ↗
  • Skillz distinguishes free virtual currency/tickets from deposit-based real-money play. Its inherited terms URL returns a branded 404. Working official FAQ ↗
  • Papaya separates free Gems competitions from cash competitions. No free-to-withdrawable-cash path is confirmed here. Official FAQ ↗
  • Wergle Flomp is free with cash prizes, but prohibits AI-generated text; limited error feedback and disclosure rules apply. Contest rules ↗
  • Sony’s cash awards are in the Series and Single Image tracks. Computer-generated content cannot be the origin of an entry. Single Image rules ↗ Series rules ↗
  • ARC has an imminent 2 November submission deadline and 8 November paper deadline; reproducible open-source solutions are required, with no internet/model APIs during Kaggle evaluation. Entry fee is not confirmed in the inspected organizer page. Organizer rules ↗
  • GEMS has a $300,000 cash pool and U.S. individual/entity eligibility restrictions. Its explicit entry-fee and AI-assistance policies remain blank in this audit. Challenge ↗ Official PDF ↗

Audit limitations — visible by design

31 official pages were inspected. This is not a full line-by-line re-verification of every inherited claim or every external link. Every original row was normalized and checked for required provenance; unsupported operational fields were removed from the comparison dataset and preserved only in the labeled historical archive. Source-page inspection does not mean all chunks, PDFs, legal terms or current account screens were inspected.

Inherited source candidates not yet checked remain flagged. Twelve source records lack a source explicitly tagged official in the upstream dataset; a signup URL is not proof of authority. Forty-five historical exclusions lack official links in their upstream exclusion records. No unsupported values were promoted to fill gaps.
