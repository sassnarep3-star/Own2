Executive summary

Free cash-prize competition ledger — project summary

Prepared 2026-11-01 · data reviewed upstream 2026-08-19 · 206 listings · source: karagemop466-tech/FreeMoneyApps

Goal

Collect, organize and analyze a full list of free-to-enter competitions that pay real cash, and publish it as a clean, mobile-friendly dashboard where every value carries an official source link and anything unconfirmable is blank and flagged — so a human (and an AI agent) can pick the best competitions to spend time on.

What exists

  • 206 listings across 9 categories, 189 with a true free-USD path, 163 active, 27 ended/out-of-season.
  • 194 rows carry an official source; 12 rows have none and are flagged with blank values on Flagged for Review.
  • Upstream audit removed 45 sweepstakes/casino/gambling-operator entries by policy (Analysis lists them with reasons).

What this build adds

  • Responsive dashboard: landing page + one subpage per category, table format, search/status/free-path filters, mobile card layout below 720px (the upstream site was a single 206-card scroll with no filters — the top complaint).
  • Verification pipeline: scripts/check-data.mjs (structural audit), scripts/check-links.mjs (every URL in data + pages, run in CI), spot checks of featured entries against official pages (verification-log.md).
  • Analysis: distributions, transparency-scored top-15 ranking with published formula, policy exclusions, patterns (Analysis).
  • Flag page: every unverified row and every broken link, with source links (Flagged for Review).

Irregularities for review

  1. 12 listings have only affiliate/forum sources (names in Flagged for Review) — values blanked, awaiting official confirmation.
  2. 3 of 9 spot-checked featured entries could not be confirmed from their official pages (Devpost — official page now lists a $1,000,000 event vs the ledger's stated range; Fanatics Survivor — official page shows no contest and third parties describe the $1M as FanCash, not USD; Dirty Dozen — official URL redirects to a generic games index). Prize values blanked + flagged, not rewritten. 6 of 9 verified exactly (ESPN NFL $102K/58 prizes, ESPN CFB $86K/46 prizes, CBS $1K×18 + $5K + $100K, ARC Prize $2M/3 tracks, Kaggle, DrivenData). Details: reports/spot-checks.json and verification-log.md.
  3. 27 ended entries keep historical prize figures — re-confirm before entering.
  4. Link check (CI run 41316899973): 326 URLs — 258 ok, 40 blocked, 26 broken. All broken/blocked URLs are published on the flag page with listing ids; none were rewritten without a confirmed official replacement (reports/link-check.json).

Bottom line

189 competitions currently advertise a genuine free path to cash. Start from the home page top-15 table, open the official rules from the row's source cell, and treat every prize figure as "per the official page as of 2026-08-19" until you re-check it yourself.
