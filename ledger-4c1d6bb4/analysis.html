Priority score — full formula

Used only to rank the "Best places to spend your time" table on the home page. It is a transparent heuristic over ledger fields, not a prediction of winnings, and it ignores prize size (prize text is not machine-parseable and guessing would violate the no-inference rule).

  • Status: active +3, mixed +1, caution −1 (ended/inactive excluded)
  • Free path: free-USD +3, some free +1, fee-required −2
  • Verification: account-only +2, eligibility check +1, KYC/tax 0
  • No official source → row excluded from ranking entirely

Reproduce it: node scripts/build-pages.mjs (function priorityScore).

Patterns worth knowing before you invest time

  • Free rooms rarely pay dollars. Skill apps (Solitaire/Bingo/Skillz-style) pay gems or tickets on free paths; withdrawable USD almost always needs a paid entry.
  • Seasonal sports games dominate volume (129 of 206 rows) and most go dormant in the off-season — check the status badge first.
  • Tax paperwork starts at $600 in the US; keep records for every win.
  • Geo-restrictions are common — state/country blocks appear in each row's "Blocked / limits" detail.
  • 12 rows have no official source and are excluded from ranking; they are published on the Flagged for Review page with their non-official links so a human can confirm or drop them.
