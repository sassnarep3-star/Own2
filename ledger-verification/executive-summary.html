Bottom line

This is a usable catalogue of 206 free-to-enter competitions with real prizes across 9 categories. It is not yet a verified list. Only 11 rows have been checked on an official page, and several important ledger claims are wrong or out of date. Use the shortlist as a starting point and check each official rule before you enter.

Findings

  • The source ledger (206 listings, last checked 2026-08-19) is internally consistent with its own totals: 189 entries are "full free path", 163 are "live", and the 62 iOS figure reconciles. But its data is 74 days old.
  • Only 11 of 206 rows (5.3%) were checked on an official page in this pass. The other 195 rows have blank prize values and are flagged as unverified.
  • Of the 11 rows checked: 3 verified, 3 partially verified, 4 in conflict with the official page, and 1 broken.
  • Policy conflict: the source excludes sportsbook, casino and real-money DFS contests. Fanatics Sportsbook, Andy's Bet Club and Gambling.com (casino games page) are still listed, and two of them are in the ledger's featured list.
  • Broken link: Dirty Dozen (gambling.com) redirects to a casino games page. No contest is shown.
  • Status conflicts: draft-pro is marked live, but its operator release says entries closed before 23 April 2026. The MLB Beat the Streak official rules end the contest with the 2026 regular season, which has passed.
  • Prize conflicts: Checkmate Gaming shows $40 to $1,500 per free tournament, not the ledger's $10 to $180. Kaggle live prize pools reach $450,000, not "$5,000 to $100,000+".
  • 13 third-party league entries carry links to another operator’s official site or to a third-party media site (R10). Those links are misleading until fixed.
  • No LICENSE exists in the upstream repo. Reuse rights for the listing data are unconfirmed.

What was checked, and what is confirmed

  • Conflict with official page Kaggle Competitions — Official page lists live prize pools of $35,000 to $450,000. The ledger says 'commonly $5,000 to $100,000+'. The ledger range is understated. Official values shown instead.
  • Partially verified Devpost Hackathons — Listing page shows live hackathons with prizes from $2,500 (Learn AR with SPECS) to $1,000,000 (Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026). The ledger lower bound of $10,000 is not supported by this sample. The ledger's top value ($685,000) is not on this page. Prize blank for the ledger range.
  • Verified ARC Prize (via Kaggle) — Homepage states 'Now $2,000,000 in prizes!' for ARC Prize 2026 and 'In partnership with Kaggle'.
  • Verified ESPN NFL Pick'em — Page states 58 prizes worth $102K, event dates 09/09/2026 to 01/10/2027, and status LIVE. Ledger link /make-picks returns the /picks page.
  • Verified ESPN College Football Pick'em — Page states 46 prizes worth $86K, event dates 09/05/2026 to 12/06/2026, and status LIVE. Ledger link /make-picks returns the /picks page.
  • Partially verified CBS Sports Pro Football Pick'em — Official rules (2026): free to play, no purchase or entry fee; sign-up from 20 Jul 2026; Challenge period 9 Sep 2026 to 10 Jan 2027; 18 weekly periods. Prize amounts are in later sections of the rules, which were not read, so the ledger prize figure ($1K/week, $5K season, $100K perfect) is unverified and left blank.
  • Conflict with official page The Draft Pro — Perfect Draft Challenge — Operator press release (PR Newswire, 8 Apr 2026): $1,000,000 Perfect Draft for the 2026 NFL Draft; entries close before the 23 Apr 2026 first round. The ledger lists status 'live' and also says the $1M closed 23 Apr 2026. The current $10,000 NBA/NHL prize is not verified. Prize blank; status needs review.
  • Broken link Dirty Dozen (Gambling.com) — Ledger link https://www.gambling.com/games/dirty-dozen redirects to https://www.gambling.com/games, a free online casino games page. No Dirty Dozen contest appears. The operator is a gambling site, which conflicts with the source's own exclusion of casino products. Also featured on the ledger homepage. Prize blank.
  • Conflict with official page MLB Beat the Streak — Prize confirmed. Official rules say the contest starts about 24 Mar 2026 and ends 'upon conclusion of the final game of the 2026 MLB regular season'. The ledger still says live (checked 2026-08-19). The official end condition has passed as of 2026-11-01. Status needs review.
  • Partially verified Metaculus Tournaments — Page states 'You can participate for free and win cash prizes for being accurate' and lists prize pools from $3,400 to $50,000. The ledger lower bound of $3,000 is not shown on the page sample. Prize blank for the ledger range.
  • Conflict with official page Checkmate Gaming (CMG) Free Tournaments — Page states 'Totally free to enter with real cash prizes'. The ledger range '$10 to $180' is contradicted by the page, which shows $40 to $1,500 on live listings. The ledger also says the platform runs paid-entry wager matches; those are not on this page.
