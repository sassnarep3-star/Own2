How to decide where to compete

  1. Establish explicit free entry and a withdrawable cash award from current event rules.
  2. Verify age, citizenship, residency, exclusions, deadlines, tax and identity requirements.
  3. Check whether coding assistance, pretrained models, external data and APIs are permitted. Do not treat silence as permission.
  4. Estimate your own compute cost and available time only after building a rules-compliant baseline. No evidence supports win odds or earnings rankings here.

Nothing on this site enters competitions, creates accounts or submits work for you. No deposit or payment workflow is provided.
