Research / The standard

Evidence first. Always.

What a sourced field means, what it does not mean, and how to reproduce this publication.

Our verification contract

  1. Official sources only for facts. Prize, fee, deadline, eligibility, AI rules, payment and deliverables must cite the organizer, official rules or designated hosting platform. The legacy ledger is provenance, not verification.
  2. Unknown means empty. Unconfirmed values are null in JSON, empty in CSV and blank in the table. Review reasons say what remains unverified. No estimated cashout limits, dates, probabilities or earnings.
  3. Field-level, not blanket, evidence. Each populated field links directly to its supporting official source. “Source inspected” describes an observation, not a certification of every claim or personal eligibility.
  4. Free + cash filter. An explicit free-entry statement plus a cash award must be sourced. Cash-equivalent substitutions, bonus balances and equipment-only prizes are excluded from this strict view. A positive result is still not a payout or eligibility guarantee.
  5. AI rules are not interchangeable. Human assistance, AutoML, bot participation and generated creative work are different permissions. A conditional allowance is not permission for all automation. Missing AI rules stay blank.
  6. Deadlines are exact where available. Source date/time wording is retained. Unknown timezones and years are not inferred. A sort date is used only where a full calendar date was explicitly available. Availability is a dated snapshot, not a real-time feed.
  7. No double-counting. Platform and event entries may overlap, as do headline pools and track awards. There is no aggregate prize total. A pool is not an individual winner’s award.

How to use the dashboard

Use the Free + cash sourced tab for the strict shortlist, select an evidence or AI filter, and expand Details & verification for eligibility, payment, deliverables and flags. Empty cells are intentional. Saved records live only in this browser’s local storage. CSV exports contain the full normalized inventory, not just the current filtered page.

Coverage and link checks

The build validates every normalized row, evidence reference, source URL format, category, date, internal link and published asset. Browser tests exercise mobile layouts, filtering, pagination, saved records and accessibility. Unchecked inherited external links may still be broken. These checks do not prove every external website is reachable or every legacy claim is correct. The official-source notebook describes exactly which pages/chunks were inspected. Unchecked external links are visibly labeled source candidates. Known broken links are retained as text in findings, not as actionable source links.

Reproduce or update

Run npm run build and npm test. The site is generated without third-party build dependencies. Curated fields and source excerpts are in data/findings.json; the untouched historical claim archive is in research/upstream.json. The generator produces category subpages, a full JSON/CSV export and a line-by-line audit. Editing imported historical claims does not verify them.

Independence and safety

No affiliate links, sponsors, signups, analytics, payment collection or contest entries are made by this site. No tax threshold or legal eligibility is inferred. Consult the official rules before registering, uploading confidential work or spending on tools. Prize values, rules and availability can change after this snapshot.
