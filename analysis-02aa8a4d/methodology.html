Verification policy

  • Every published value carries a verbatim quote and the official URL it was read from (check date 2026-11-02, or an earlier attributed pass).
  • If a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, the cell stays blank and is stamped unverified. Values are never inferred or filled in.
  • A reachable page is not verification of a competition; only the quoted content counts.
  • Official rules win every argument. Promotional claims that conflict with rules are flagged, not adopted.
  • Every table row links to at least one source, or is explicitly stamped no official source.

Tiers: A = score ≥70 and free entry + cash prize + open status all confirmed; B = promising but at least one of the three is unconfirmed (score ≥50); C = weak/long-shot on current evidence; X = excluded (not free, or prize is not withdrawable cash, or closed); unranked = insufficient evidence — never estimated.

Analyst-rated components (cadence, eligibility, effort) are judgments derived from the quoted format text, not claims made by the source. They are shown with their basis on the ranked page and capped so they cannot outweigh the evidence-gated components (free/cash/open = 65 of 100 points).

What this pass did not do

  • No contest accounts were created, no entries submitted, no deposits made, no prizes claimed.
  • Chunked pages were read in the chunks disclosed in the coordination log; uninspected chunks are not treated as evidence.
  • External link availability is partial (sample recheck only); the full 326-URL baseline is linked from Link health.
  • Legal, tax and personal-eligibility qualification is out of scope — official rules win every argument.
