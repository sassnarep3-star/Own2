Purpose

Collect, organize, and analyze free-to-enter competitions that pay real cash prizes, so users can find the best competitions to spend their time on. Entries are organized by category in a simple, dashboard-style table, and every row links to the operator’s own official signup or rules page. This is a research desk, not a sponsor: official rules win every argument.

Source of data

All 206 entries are taken from the Free Money & Contest Ledger (https://karagemop466-tech.github.io/FreeMoneyApps/), pass S, reviewed 2026-08-19, which itself sources each listing from the operator’s own rules or game page wherever possible. Each source carries a tier: official / affiliate / forum.

Key findings

  • 189 of 207 listings have a fully free-to-enter path (91%).
  • 153 listings are live and fully free — these are the best candidates to spend time on.
  • Fantasy & sports picks is the largest category (129 entries), dominated by free ESPN/CBS pick’em and survivor contests.
  • Skill-tournament apps (Papaya, Skillz, Pocket7Games) mostly require an entry fee for cash; free rooms pay gems, not dollars — treat them as paid-entry, not free-USD.
  • US tax forms apply at $600+ (W-9); non-US winners may need a W-8BEN. Taxes are the winner’s responsibility.
  • Nobody legitimate will ask you to pay a fee to collect a prize. Bonus Cash / gems are not withdrawable USD.

Verification policy

Every table row links to its source(s). Where a value (prize, cash-out floor, payout method, eligibility) cannot be confirmed from the operator’s official page, it is left blank or shown as-is from the ledger and the row is flagged as unverified. No values are inferred or filled in. Affiliate and forum sources are shown as secondary, never as the sole basis for a prize figure.
