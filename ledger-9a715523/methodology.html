HOW TO READ THIS SITE

Method & verification limits

A link is a pointer. A quote is evidence. Neither is a guarantee that you can enter or get paid.

Collection process

  1. Read the shared coordination board and claim a non-overlapping namespace.
  2. Pin the upstream repository commit; retain all 206 identities and nine categories.
  3. Do not copy old operational claims into current fact cells.
  4. Open organizer or official administrator pages; record exact excerpts, locators and URLs.
  5. Leave missing or conflicting values blank and publish the issue in the review queue.
  6. Generate static pages and test row coverage, evidence, links, CSV and browser behavior.

Ranking policy

No fake “winability” score, hourly rate or expected-value estimate. Prize pools are not comparable to individual awards, and entrant counts are usually unknown.

The featured order is an editorial research sequence based on explicit agent rules, stated submission requirements and runway. Filters let you choose by category, evidence and agent policy. Top cash award and total pool are kept separate.

Country names are reproduced only when the official rule lists them. “Worldwide” is never inferred from a working website.

Time, money and automation

Free entry means the cited rule does not require an entry purchase or fee. It does not eliminate internet, compute, hardware, travel, tax or withdrawal costs. A membership gate with unknown cost is not treated as a confirmed zero-cost opportunity.

AI assistance is evaluated separately from automated entry. A contest can require an AI product while prohibiting an outside agent from authoring or submitting the entry. Where permission is unclear, the site says so. No automated contest entry, deposit, account creation or prize claim was performed.

Source coverage and freshness

The static audit date is 2 November 2026. “Published window open” is a date comparison against the cited rule, not a live check of the entry form. Event pages and rules can change without notice. The source notebook identifies the passages read; unread chunks are not evidence.

External reachability results, when present, are a separate network observation. A 403, timeout or TLS failure is inconclusive; a 200 response can still be a stale, wrong or non-cash offer. All such limitations are non-blocking review items, not hidden assumptions.
