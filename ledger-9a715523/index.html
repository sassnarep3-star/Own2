DO NOT SKIP

Three checks before entry

  1. 1
    Can you enter?

    Residency, age, membership and team restrictions.

  2. 2
    Is it really cash?

    Separate money from credits, gift cards and conditional jackpots.

  3. 3
    Can an agent help?

    AI-themed does not mean agent-authored entries are allowed.
