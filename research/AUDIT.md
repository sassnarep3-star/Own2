- Skillz old terms link returns a branded 404. Actionable links use the working official homepage, not an invented replacement terms URL.
- AIcrowd extraction contains unrelated spam/HTML-looking categories. No such material was executed or trusted.

- Original date badges are historical. The source header says Pass C / 17 Aug while data reports Pass S / 19 Aug 2026. No freshness claims inherited.
- Source landing page includes a blanket $600 US tax claim. It is not adopted; only contest-specific official tax-document requirements are reproduced.
